YALE–UCONN BIOMEDICAL RESEARCH ATLAS — EDITION 10
Core dataset reviewed October 28, 2026; clinical layer reviewed October 30, 2026

OPEN biomedical-atlas.html in a modern browser. It is a self-contained offline web
app; source links require internet. No installation or server is needed.

176 curated profiles: Yale 18, UConn Health 37, UConn Storrs 108, regional campuses 8, JAX-affiliated 5.
Biology Programs: MCB 43, EEB 32, PNB 25; 50 profiles added in this update.
All 15 domains; 590 shared-topic edges.
PNB faculty audit 1 November 2026: all 25 current directory entries included.
Directory-only entries do not establish active labs. Six emeritus-category and two
in-memoriam entries remain in the separate coverage audit.
632 unique matched publications and 1044 unique funding records.
Publication and funding counts are source-search results, not an exhaustive census.

STUDENT WORK
Use Student work to search names, projects, methods and named mentors. Individual
work descriptions distinguish interests, planned work and described projects.
Missing descriptions remain unspecified; lab-wide methods and prior-lab experience
are not attributed to individuals. Membership conflicts/stale sources are flagged.
No project dates, completed tasks, hours or publication ownership are inferred.
Student-work annotations do not change the existing activity scores.

PARETO RESEARCH ACTIVITY
Compare uncapped listed-student counts, documented engagement years, activity-type
breadth and optionally verified volunteer counts. No weighted total is used.
A dominates B only if at least as high on every selected axis and higher on one.
The observed frontier and subsequent layers are recomputed within current filters.
Unknown values are excluded, never zero-filled. At least two axes and two complete
records are required. The default three-axis comparison currently has only one
complete record, so no frontier is assigned. A two-axis student/breadth exploration
is available by deselecting engagement. Volunteer counts are all unverified.
This compares documented evidence, not true activity, quality, or mentoring.
The scatterplot is a student/breadth projection; colors reflect all selected axes.
Legacy weighted totals survive only as explicitly labeled export history.

CLINICAL ACCESS EVIDENCE
Use Clinical access and the sidebar Clinical sample evidence filter. Separate columns
show medical credentials, documented patient care, clinical positions, named collection
routes, specimen use, patient-derived models, publication leads and hospital context.
Select evidence cells for source links, dates, and publication author roles.
Coverage is selective: an empty cell or unreviewed degree does not mean no access.
No inventory, recruitment, permission to share, or probability of access is asserted.
Institutional campus context is not an individual hospital appointment or measured
distance. Historical partnerships do not establish current sample availability.
Clinical title/type leads can include reviews or secondary analyses. Topic edges
remain unchanged; neither proximity nor coauthorship creates collaboration links.

TECHNOLOGY MAP
20 technology/resource categories, 12 selectively reviewed labs with 32 direct
source entries, and 67 title-based publication leads. There is evidence for 27
researchers in total. This is a first curated layer, not a complete lab census.
Use Technology map and the sidebar Technology / resource filter. Default evidence
is Lab / institutional sources; include publication leads to broaden discovery.
Select D or P matrix cells for sources. P means a title-based lead, not a confirmed
capability. Authorship never proves who performed the technique or owns equipment.
Five shared facilities are listed separately. Patient/donor research use does not
mean samples are available for sharing. Source dates and access notes are retained.
Lab-source evidence may be historical and is not restricted to selected journals.

AUTHORSHIP AND PI ROLES
The Researcher role filter separates first, last, middle coauthor and sole author
positions. Positions are sourced from ordered indexed author metadata. Last author
does not establish PI or corresponding-author status; equal contributions and
correspondence are not systematically verified. Funding separately identifies
contact PI, multiple PI, subproject PI, fellowship PI, co-investigator and other
reported support. Filters match each researcher before records are deduplicated.

PUBLICATIONS
Window: October 28, 2021 through October 28, 2026 inclusive.
Science, Nature, Cell research-journal families plus an explicit selection of major
journals. The exact allowlist and date/identity rules are in research-methods.json
and About the evidence. Journal inclusion is a policy choice, not a measured impact
factor threshold. No unrestricted Springer Nature or Elsevier publisher inclusion.
All verified matches from the indexed searches are included, with pagination in the
app. Indexing delays, author names, missing affiliations, and source coverage limit
completeness. Unresolved identities are excluded and retained in the search audit.
Older illustrative papers remain separately labeled as topic-provenance evidence.

FUNDING
Awards overlapping the same five-year window. Federal award records retain their
budget/project dates, identifiers, recorded PI role and fiscal-year amount basis. Older fiscal years can qualify through overlapping budgets.
Amounts are award totals, not personal allocations or whole-project lifetime totals.
Shared awards are deduplicated in the Funding view; subproject amounts are suppressed.
Other-funder evidence is supplemental and includes source-labeled announcements,
institutional grants, investigator awards and philanthropic support. Unknown amounts
or dates remain unknown. Announcement dates are not automatically award start dates.
No verified match does not mean no funding; co-investigator awards may be missing.

USE
Filter researchers by institution, domain, topic, or name. Publications and Funding
tabs respect these filters and have title/identifier, journal/funder, and year filters.
Choose a researcher for a profile and then View all for that person's records.
Use Show next to load every record. Clear record filters removes the person scope.
About the evidence includes methods. Download data exports the full dataset.
Graph lines mean shared curated research topics, never inferred collaborations.
Layout positions and node sizes do not rank researchers. JAX affiliation is labeled
separately and does not imply a Yale or UConn appointment.

LIMITATIONS
Selective faculty coverage, not all faculty at these institutions. Metadata and
institutional sources were checked; full texts were not systematically reviewed.
Topics are editorial classifications. Historical work can predate current affiliation.
No clinical efficacy or current trial recruitment is inferred. The atlas is independent
and not endorsed by these institutions. Hosted at
https://bioxagi.github.io/biomedical-atlas/; the standalone file also works offline.
